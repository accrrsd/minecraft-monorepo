# Renewable Calcite, Tuff & Deepslate

Stop treating Calcite, Tuff, and Deepslate as non-renewable resources! This datapack provides balanced, vanilla-friendly crafting recipes to obtain these building blocks without tearing up the underground landscape or strip-mining entire biomes. Perfect for vanilla survival, skyblock, and factory/automation playstyles.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💧 Calcite
* **Bulk (8:1)**: **8 Bone Blocks** + **1 Water Bucket** (shaped) ➔ **8 Calcite**
* **Single (1:1)**: **1 Bone Block** + **1 Water Bucket** (shapeless) ➔ **1 Calcite**

### 🔥 Tuff
* **Bulk (8:1)**: **8 Mud Blocks** + **1 Lava Bucket** (shaped) ➔ **8 Tuff**
* **Single (1:1)**: **1 Mud Block** + **1 Lava Bucket** (shapeless) ➔ **1 Tuff**

### 🌋 Deepslate
* **Bulk (8:1)**: **8 Stone Blocks** + **1 Lava Bucket** (shaped) ➔ **8 Deepslate**
* **Single (1:1)**: **1 Stone Block** + **1 Lava Bucket** (shapeless) ➔ **1 Deepslate**

*(Empty buckets are always returned upon crafting!)*

---

## 📖 Recipe Book Integration
All recipes automatically unlock in your Recipe Book as soon as you collect any of the required materials.
